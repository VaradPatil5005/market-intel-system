# Digital Asset Strategy Brief: Global Stablecoin Regulation & Institutional Order Flow
**Date**: November 2025 | **Asset Classes**: Digital Assets, Commercial Banking | **Classification**: CONFIDENTIAL

## Regulatory Milestones
Implementation of the EU Markets in Crypto-Assets (MiCA) regulatory perimeter alongside US Clarity for Payment Stablecoins legislative framework required commercial issuers to back circulating tokens 1:1 with short-duration sovereign Treasury bills (under 90 days maturity) and central bank reserve deposits.

## Structural Market Implications
1. **Sovereign Debt Demand Absorption**: Stablecoin treasury reserves emerged as the 14th largest holder of US Treasuries globally, absorbing over $140B in short-duration T-bills and flattening front-end curve yields.
2. **Bitcoin & Ethereum Liquidity Confluence**: Spot exchange reserves hit multi-year lows as institutional custody solutions absorbed daily block order flows. Microstructure analysis revealed sustained negative funding rates on perpetual futures during delta-neutral cash-and-carry basis trades.
3. **Volatility Regimes**: 30-day realized volatility compressed toward equity-index regimes, prompting systematic volatility-targeting funds to increase gross exposure allocations.
